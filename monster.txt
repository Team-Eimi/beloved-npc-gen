Monster generator:
**Basic**: Choose level (5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80)
- selected level varies +/- 5 (aka a level 5 area ranges levels 1-10). Define this value as LEVEL
Outputs:
STR
Attack
CON
Defense
HP
AGI
Dodge
INT
Accuracy
Resist
CHA
MAG
Power
Mana
All stats are determined by: LEVEL = 1d4xLEVEL (you can RNG 1-4). All substats are stat/5, except for Accuracy, which is INT/5 + AGI/5
Magic is enabled only by an extra that explicitly enables MAG. Otherwise show MAG, Power, and Mana as Not Applicable and exclude MAG from EXP. EXP is (STR + CON + INT + AGI + CHA + MAG if magical) / 5, rounded down. Minimum HP is 1.
